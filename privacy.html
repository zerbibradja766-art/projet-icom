At RADJA, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website and how that information may be used.

01

Introduction

Welcome to RADJA. This Privacy Policy applies to visitors and customers who access or use our online store.

By using our website, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. Please review it to understand our privacy practices.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our website, the following types of information may be requested or collected:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Delivery address and order details.
  • Messages or information you send through our contact form.
  • Basic technical information about website usage, where applicable.

We aim to request only information that is relevant to providing our store services.

03

How We Use Your Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Processing and managing customer orders.
  • Contacting customers about their purchases.
  • Responding to questions and support requests.
  • Improving our website and shopping experience.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
04

Information Sharing

RADJA does not intend to sell customers' personal information.

Information may be shared when necessary to provide requested services, such as with delivery or payment service providers, or when required by applicable law.

Any sharing should be limited to what is necessary for the relevant purpose.

05

Cookies

Cookies are small files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website.

They can help websites remember certain preferences and understand how visitors use their pages.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work as expected if cookies are disabled.

06

Data Security

We aim to take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

However, no website or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07

Data Retention

Information may be kept for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, meet business requirements or comply with applicable law.

08

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including:

  • Requesting access to your personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion where legally applicable.
  • Asking questions about how your information is used.

Contact RADJA if you would like to make a privacy request.

09

External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. These websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies.

We encourage you to review the privacy information of any external website you visit.

10

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without the required authorization.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

11

Changes to This Privacy Policy

RADJA may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

We recommend reviewing this page periodically.

12

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us.
